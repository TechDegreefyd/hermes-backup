## 📊 WHAT IS DAPA?

**DAPA = Domain Authority + Page Authority**

### Domain Authority (DA)
- Measures the authority of an **entire website** on a scale of 0-100
- Calculated by Moz based on:
  - Number of quality backlinks
  - Link authority of referring domains
  - Domain age and history
  - Content quality
  - Other signals

### Page Authority (PA)
- Measures the authority of a **specific page** on a scale of 0-100
- Helps individual pages rank higher
